{"greeting": "Hello from Compose!", "count": 9, "hostname": "c01b588592c8", "storage": "redis"}

$ docker compose down

$ docker compose up -d --wait

$ curl -fsS http://localhost:8080/ (после пересоздания)
{"greeting": "Hello from Compose!", "count": 9, "hostname": "c55a1a0833ff", "storage": "redis"}
